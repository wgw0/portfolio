**Project:** William Woods Personal Website
**Repository:** https://github.com/wgw0/portfolio

---

# 1. Overview

## Objective

Redesign the existing portfolio website from a traditional software engineering portfolio into a modern professional website that reflects my career as a Senior Analyst working across technology, strategy, organisational improvement, analytics, and consulting.

The new website should communicate how I think, solve problems and work with organisations rather than showcase programming languages or technical projects.

The overall feel should be comparable to a boutique management consultancy or strategy consultant's personal website rather than a software developer portfolio.

The site should appear mature, understated and highly professional.

---

# 2. Target Audience

Primary audience:

* Consulting recruiters
* Consulting partners
* Hiring managers
* Directors
* Government organisations
* Defence organisations
* Senior client stakeholders
* Professional network

Secondary audience:

* Prospective clients
* Colleagues
* Conference attendees

# 3. Design Principles

The website should communicate:

* Professionalism
* Clarity
* Confidence
* Intelligence
* Curiosity
* Simplicity
* Structured thinking

Avoid anything that feels overly "tech startup", "developer portfolio", or "creative agency".

# 25. Overall Impression

After visiting the site, the visitor should conclude:

* William understands technology but focuses on business outcomes.
* He is comfortable working with senior stakeholders.
* He approaches problems methodically.
* He has experience across multiple sectors.
* He communicates clearly.
* He is interested in organisational improvement and change rather than simply building software.

The website should reinforce a professional identity aligned with consulting, change and transformation, and technology-enabled organisational improvement, rather than software engineering. Every design decision, piece of content, and interaction should support this positioning.
